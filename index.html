Safety boundaries & language

Four-state output language, used consistently across every screening domain.

Results are screening signals, not pathogen or substance identification. Analyzer Elite Pro is a field screening aid intended to flag conditions that warrant further attention. It does not diagnose, and it does not replace certified laboratory confirmation.

Normal No elevated signal detected in this domain during this run.
Caution / Retest An ambiguous signal; a repeat scan or secondary check is recommended.
Elevated reading A signal outside expected baseline; escalate per your agency's protocol.
Outside range The reading falls outside the device's validated detection envelope.

Genetic or molecular pathogen confirmation is performed off-device by a certified lab or companion system, and is reported separately from the on-device screening result.
