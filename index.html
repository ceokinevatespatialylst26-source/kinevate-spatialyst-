  • Non-Contact Safety & Efficiency

    With instant, sub-second hazard identification and multi-spectral UV-A fluorometric analysis, KINEVATE Analyzers provide rapid and highly accurate bio-chemical intelligence, critical for time-sensitive situations.

  • Rugged & Reliable Performance

    Engineered to meet IP67 standards, combined with active micro-thermoelectric temperature control, our devices are designed to perform reliably in the most extreme and demanding environments, from industrial settings to military operations.
