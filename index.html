Safety Boundaries & Language

Terms & disclosure

KINEVATE Analyzer Elite (Citizen and Pro Field/Industrial editions) is a screening aid, not a diagnostic or lab-confirmed identification device. Results describe a screening signal only, using four defined states: Normal, Caution / Retest, Elevated reading detected, and Outside validation range.

An elevated reading is a prompt to retest or seek lab-based confirmation — it is not a claim that a specific pathogen, chemical, or substance has been identified. The device is designed to fail safely: it will never report Normal when a sensor fault, calibration error, low battery, or out-of-range operating condition is present.

This page and the interactive demo above use a mock device and simulated results for demonstration purposes only.
