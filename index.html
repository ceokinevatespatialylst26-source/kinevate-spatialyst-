The answer screen

Four answers. No guesswork.

Every screening ends in one of four plain results, color-coded so a crew can act in a glance.

Normal

Readings are within the expected range for this setting.

Caution / Retest

The reading is borderline. Run the screening again to confirm.

Elevated reading

Something is above the normal range. Follow up with lab testing.

Outside range

Conditions were outside what the device can check, so no result is given.

A result is a screening signal. It tells a team when to act and when to call for lab confirmation. It does not identify a specific substance.
